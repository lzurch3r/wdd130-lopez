Welcome to bird discovery journal.

This website is designed for you to keep track of birds that you see, and keep notes and observations in a simple way.

Start Tracking Your Sightings

As you find different birds, you can add them to your personal checklist. You can also record when and where you saw each bird and write notes about anything interesting you noticed.

Build Your Collection

Over time, your checklist will grow as you discover more species. You can look back at the birds you've already found and see which ones you haven't spotted yet.

How It Works

  1. Browse the list of birds.
  2. Check off the species you've seen.
  3. Add the date, location, and your observations.
  4. Keep exploring and adding to your list.

This website will currently just have the birds in the midwest part of the US, because it would take forever to add all the species in north America.

Keep Track of Your Progress

Every time a user checks off a new species, it will be added to their personal list. The website will show how many different birds they have spotted and save their progress. They will also be able to record how many times they see each bird.

Birds Included

The list will include common backyard birds, songbirds, woodpeckers, waterfowl, birds of prey, and other species found in the Midwest.
